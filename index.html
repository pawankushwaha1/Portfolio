Hands-on Prototyping Workshop

A peek into the machining, additive manufacturing, and physical testing workflows.

01
Rapid Ideation & Drafting

Hand-drawn orthographic sketches on layout paper to establish human ergonomics and critical tolerance envelopes.

02
High-Precision SLA Resin Printing

Rigid 50-micron resin prints for mechanical snapping tests, interlocking gears, and optical clear light pipes.

03
5-Axis CNC Aluminum Machining

Billet aerospace-grade 6061 aluminum milled on high-speed CNC with bead-blasted satin anodized finishing.

04
Custom Character & Brand Art

Vector illustrations and mascot styling designed to accompany hardware unboxing and brand digital presences.

05
Additive Fabrication & Micro Extrusion

Direct drive toolhead micro-extrusion calibration for high-tolerance mechanical gears and internal snap joints.

06
Hands-on Bench Assembly

Physical fitting and tactile testing of modular parts, weighted balance, and user contact points.

07
Parametric 3D Surface CAD

High-curvature class-A surface generation in CAD ensuring seamless draft angles and parting lines.

08
Ergonomic Scale Studies

Rapid physical scale models to evaluate proportion, spatial footprint, and user interaction before full tooling.
