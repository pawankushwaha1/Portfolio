Why edits don't appear on other devices by default (and how to fix it)

Netlify is a static website host. When you edit titles, case studies, or photos in this Admin Panel, browsers save those edits inside this computer's local browser memory. Other devices (your phone, a tablet, or a client's computer) cannot see this computer's memory.

Below are the two ways to synchronize your edits across all devices worldwide:

Option A: 1-Click Netlify Publish (Free & 100% Reliable)

Download your compiled portfolio-data.json file with all your project case studies, photos, and settings ready to drop into your website folder.

How to publish to Netlify in 2 quick steps:
  1. Click "Download portfolio-data.json" below.
  2. Copy the downloaded file into your portfolio repository folder and push/upload to Netlify.
✅ That's it! Every phone, tablet, and visitor worldwide will automatically receive your updates!

Option B: Real-Time Cloud Sync (No Re-Deploy Needed!)

Connect a free cloud JSON store (like JSONBin.io) or GitHub Auto-Deploy. Edits made in the Admin Panel update all devices in real-time instantly without re-deploying!

⚡ How GitHub Auto-Deploy Works:
When you click "Save & Publish All", your updates are committed directly to your repository (pawankushwaha1/Portfolio). Netlify automatically detects the commit and updates your live website across every phone, tablet, and computer worldwide!
